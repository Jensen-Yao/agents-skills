# Idea Review Perspectives

Use perspectives to inspect different conceptual weaknesses. Consolidate their findings in the one report from `strict-idea-review.md`; separate full reports per role are unnecessary. Separate calls count as independent reviews only when actually performed and permitted by the host.

## Default Lenses

- **Problem / field:** Is the bottleneck specific and important? Does the claimed gap exist in the closest work?
- **Novelty / prior art:** Which problem, assumption, formulation, or mechanism is new? Could unfamiliar terminology be hiding an existing idea?
- **Method / logic:** Do the assumptions, components, and proposed causal or algorithmic explanation fit together? Identify a concrete contradiction rather than demanding results.
- **Contribution / audience:** What would the community learn from this idea? Is its value substantive for the intended audience?

An experiment reviewer is not a default role. Add experiment, resource, implementation, or empirical-evidence perspectives only for an explicitly requested extension. Supplying a paper that contains experiments does not itself authorize expanding a concept-only review.

## Synthesis

First establish the best-supported assessment, then check the strongest favorable interpretation and the strongest substantiated objection. These interpretations may agree. Do not assign a score first and invent reasons to defend it, force a reject case, or describe single-agent perspectives as a consensus of independent reviewers.

State development recommendation and confidence once in the report. Every objection must concern the supplied problem, assumption, mechanism, or verified prior art. An incomplete experiment package is outside default idea review.
